Round 10d (2026-10-04). OWL individuals and object properties from neurosymbolic_ecd.ttl. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click a node for its specification.

How to read this graph

A chain of evidentiary reasoning from a logged event to a component profile for one session — and a record of where that chain is strong and where it is not. Rounds 10 and 10b (2026-10-02): preconditions numbered 0, component-coded identifiers that carry no round digit, no sub-indicators, and mnemonic signal names (identifiers only; no rule changed). Since Round 9: persistence unchanged, adaptability re-specified on the four-process model with at least two indicators per process, signals recast as one attempt-level layer, and no construct-level claims.

What you are looking at

An in-task evidence model links low-level features to constructs (Kerr et al., 2016; Goldhammer et al., 2021). Every node is an OWL individual and every arrow an object property, so the model can be queried and reasoned over. It covers a robotics-simulator corpus of 690,202 events from 239 students; nothing in the environment was designed for assessment.

The columns: one job per layer

ColumnLayerWhat lives there
Log fieldsL0Raw event columns. Hidden by default.
Low-level featuresL1Episodes (RUN, CODE, RESET), the outcome telemetry tick, micro-actions and block events, labelled from log fields with no interpretation.
Behavioral signalsL2Eighteen signals with mnemonic names (sig_unsuccessful_attempt, sig_revision_cycle, ...): attempt- or episode-level patterns, named for what happened. Signals make no claim and gate nothing; each one feeds at least one indicator. This is the only layer bound per playground. Only signals sit here.
IndicatorsL3 / L415 session indicators (the Round 8c set, with Mid-Run Correction and Movement-Rule Replacement each split in two in Round 10b), 5 provisional indicators (ADA-3a, ADA-3b for Regulating; ADA-4a–ADA-4c for Relating & Resourcing; white boxes), 2 descriptive elements (grey, never scored), and 2 portfolio indicators that corroborate and never set a status. There are no sub-indicators (Round 10b): a part that carries evidence is an indicator with its own letter, and any other part is a companion column of its indicator. The session covariates (SC-01 to SC-03, recorded as context, not evidence; hidden by default) sit at the foot of this column.
Preconditions and componentsPreconditions, numbered 0 because they are conditions of the situation rather than parts of the construct: PER-C0 (a goal-directed task) and ADA-C0 (an identifiable initial approach, required for Revising only). Components: persistence PER-C1 challenge, PER-C2 sustained effort, PER-C3 completion; adaptability ADA-C1 Recognizing, ADA-C2 Revising, ADA-C3 Regulating, ADA-C4 Relating & Resourcing, numbered as Ober (2026) numbers the four processes.
ConstructsPersistence and adaptability. No construct-level claim is made for either.
Validation strategyWhat each indicator is waiting on, and which validity link it would strengthen.

What a session reports

Each session returns a component profile, not a construct claim. Every scored component is Evidenced (at least one applicable indicator meets its evidence rule), Not evidenced (an indicator is applicable but none meets its rule) or Not observable (no indicator is applicable in that playground, or its indicators depend on a log payload not yet verified). Statuses resting on provisional indicators carry a provisional tag. Preconditions are Met, Not met or Not identifiable. Each component's evidence rule is in its note: click a component node.

Patterns worth looking for

Gold edges converge on sig_unsuccessful_attempt. Persistence is effort despite challenge, so its challenge- and continuation-conditioned indicators all read the unsuccessful-attempt signal. On the adaptability side only the two Regulating indicators do, because regulation is read in response to a setback; Recognizing, Revising and Relating & Resourcing carry no antecedent.
Count the arrows into each component. PER-C1 and ADA-C3 have two indicators each; PER-C3, ADA-C1 and ADA-C4 three; PER-C2 and ADA-C2 four. The Regulating and Relating & Resourcing indicators are all provisional: Regulating is read from how a student paces their response to setbacks, which still needs validating against another source, and Relating & Resourcing depends on a UI payload not yet verified.

What this graph does not claim

These are not validated measures. Thresholds are frequency-informed heuristics with no measurement model behind them, and generalization is the weakest link in the interpretive argument. A profile describes behavioral patterns; it is not an estimate of a trait.
  • Selected does not mean validated. It means the indicator survived reduction.
  • All values are Layer 0 — deterministic functions of logged fields. Layers 1 and 2 are unused.
  • Help from teachers or classmates is invisible here. ADA-C4 can only ever reflect in-environment resources.

Try this

  • Set Trace a construct to Adaptability and follow each process back through its indicators and signals to the log fields.
  • Click sig_unsuccessful_attempt and read the incoming list: every inference that depends on an unsuccessful attempt being detectable, and why so little persistence evidence exists in the playgrounds that report no outcome.
  • Click ADA-2a. It is the one adaptability rule Round 9 changed: it now counts strategy families first used after a run has played out, so it records a revision rather than an accumulation.
  • Turn on Session covariates to see the former session-level signals, now recorded as context rather than evidence.
  • Turn on Cut candidates and unmetCriterion for the rejected space (Messick, 1989).

Terminology

TermMeaning
Round 10d (2026-10-04)Rule decision; no identifier changed. PER-3a is not applicable (NA) when the session has no unsuccessful attempt (PER-1a = 0, which records that case); it was 0. Otherwise 1 if any attempt after the longest streak is successful, 0 if none, NA without an outcome record. PER-3b unchanged (defined only when PER-3a = 1). PER-C3 is Not observable with no streak and no stated criterion; where a stated criterion applies, Evidenced if PER-3c > 0, otherwise Not evidenced. Disclosed: PER-3a = 1 implies PER-1b = 1; in Classes A and B PER-3a = 0 occurs essentially only when the session ends inside its longest streak.
Round 10c (2026-10-02)Rule decisions; no identifier changed. Class C (RoverRescue, GOMars): an attempt is unsuccessful when it is lower than the student’s previous attempt with an outcome record (decision D3 now open only for the quantity). PER-PF1a: a return after an unresolved session (last RUN followed by code editing and no further RUN), the one rule in every playground; it reads no outcome record. PER-3c and CRIT-1 are rates over the attempts with an outcome record. Neutral wording throughout: a session is unresolved and an attempt unsuccessful, describing the episode rather than the student.
Round 10b (2026-10-02)Sub-indicators retired: ADA-1a.1/1a.2 became ADA-1a Mid-Run Intervention Rate and ADA-1b Mid-Run Correction, Stop-and-Revise became ADA-1c, ADA-2c.2/2c.1 became ADA-2c Within-Family Revision and ADA-2d Cross-Family Replacement; other parts became companion columns. PER-PF1 → PER-PF1a, ADA-PF1 → ADA-PF2a. New signal sig_midrun_then_success; sig_movement_replacement split into within- and cross-family. No rule changed.
Round 10 (2026-10-02)Identifiers only. Preconditions numbered 0 and typed as preconditions; components renumbered (ADA-C1 to ADA-C4 = Ober’s processes 1-4); indicators component-coded with no round digit; signals given mnemonic names. Crosswalk in Appendix C of the extraction rules and in the Round10 node.
Round 9 (2026-10-01)Four-process adaptability; signals recast twelve behavioral signals (four further signals (sig_rapid_unrevised_rerun, sig_interattempt_interval, sig_resource_informed_revision, sig_feedback_tool_opened) added in Round 9b with the provisional Regulating and Relating & Resourcing indicators) (crosswalk in each signal's note and in Appendix C of the extraction rules); Focal tier dissolved; BS-F2 and BS-S9 retired; BS-S10 to BS-S12 became session covariates; BS-S2 to BS-S7 moved to dashboard diagnostics outside the evidence model; component profiles replace construct claims.
Unsuccessful attemptA RUN whose outcome meets its playground's rule: weight_cleared == 0 in Class A, the trash-and-coral composite in Class B, a within-student relative rule in Class C, not observable in Class D. It describes one attempt, not the student.
IdentifiersAn indicator’s number is its component and there is no round digit: PER-2b evidences PER-C2, ADA-0 discharges ADA-C0. Letters run from the simplest and most portable derivation to the least (ADA-1a rate, ADA-1b correction, ADA-1c stop-and-revise). Portfolio indicators put PF before the component number (PER-PF1a, ADA-PF2a); the outcome criterion is CRIT-1. There are no sub-indicators: since Round 10b side quantities are companion columns of their indicator (e.g. ADA_1c_stop_rate, PER_PF1a_returned). Signals carry mnemonic names (sig_*), which are also the data columns.
PreconditionsPER-C0 is required for every persistence component; ADA-C0 only for ADA-C2 Revising, since a shift cannot be observed without a starting point. Each is discharged by one descriptive element (PER-0, ADA-0) and reported Met, Not met or Not identifiable.

Working with the files

neurosymbolic_ecd.ttl and .owl are the same ontology in two serializations. Run a reasoner and ConativelyConditionedIndicator populates with the sig_unsuccessful_attempt-conditioned persistence indicators. validation_queries.rq reproduces the audits, including the Round 9 layering checks. worked_example_WREN-C042_S001.ttl traces one session to its profile.
